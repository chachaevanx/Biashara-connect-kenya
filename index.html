About our platform

Helping Kenyans discover and support local businesses.

Biashara Connect Kenya is a business directory concept created to make it easier for people to find shops, products and services across the country. We aim to help customers discover their options and help businesses reach more people.

From a furniture shop in Nakuru to a salon in Kisumu or a hotel in Mombasa, our goal is to make local business discovery simpler and more accessible.
